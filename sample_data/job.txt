Junior Data Analyst — Northgate Retail Group, Leeds (Hybrid)

Northgate Retail Group is one of the UK's fastest-growing value retailers,
with 240 stores nationwide. We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join
our commercial analytics team and turn sales data into decisions that reach
the shop floor.

Key responsibilities
- Analyse weekly sales data across 240 stores and produce clear reports for
  buyers and store managers.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau to track sales,
  stock availability and promotions.
- Write SQL queries to extract data from our data warehouse and clean it
  with Python and pandas.
- Run A/B testing on promotions and present findings to stakeholders.
- Support forecasting and budgeting for the trading team.
- Present insights in plain English to non-technical colleagues.

Essential requirements
- A degree in a numerate subject (mathematics, statistics, economics or similar).
- Strong Excel skills, including pivot tables and VLOOKUP.
- Experience with SQL and Python for data analysis.
- Excellent communication skills and attention to detail.
- A problem-solving mindset and the ability to work in a team under deadlines.

Desirable skills
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI dashboards.
- Knowledge of Google Analytics and statistics.
- Understanding of data visualisation best practice.
- Experience with data warehousing or ETL pipelines.
- Time management and adaptability in a fast-paced retail environment.

We offer a salary of £28,000–£32,000, 25 days' holiday, hybrid working
(3 days in Leeds, 2 from home) and a clear path to Data Analyst within 18 months.
